# Season passes and player level / XP in casual puzzle games

Research date: 2026-10-23. Every page below was seen on that date unless a line says otherwise.

Labels:
- **FACT**: seen on the source itself (the game's help center, the store page, an official blog).
- **REPORTED**: a secondary source (fan wiki, blog, analyst, forum). Treat as likely but not certain.
- **ESTIMATE**: my own inference. No source says it.

## Türkçe özet

- İlk 6 bulmaca oyununun 6'sında da aylık bir pass var. Hepsi aynı kalıbı izliyor: yaklaşık 30 basamak, ana bölümleri kazanınca dolan bir anahtar ya da rozet sayacı, ücretsiz ve ücretli iki sıra, ücretli tarafta 5 yerine 8 can, altın isim ve çerçeve. Fiyatlar 4,99 $ (Gardenscapes), 9,99 $ (Royal Match, Royal Kingdom, Toon Blast Golden) ve 19,99 $ (Toon Blast Diamond).
- Büyük match-3 oyunlarında ayrı bir XP ya da oyuncu seviyesi bulunamadı. Oyuncunun görünen seviyesi harita seviyesi. XP sistemleri bulmaca-RPG, bingo ve strateji oyunlarında var.
- Pass'in tutundurmaya ve gelire etkisini sayıyla gösteren açık bir kaynak bulunamadı. Analistler pass'in "dönüşümü artırdığını ama satın alma derinliği düşükse diğer satışları yediğini" söylüyor; örnek olarak Clash Royale veriliyor.
- XP kaynakları için öneri (TAHMİN): Bölüm kazanmak ve zor bölüm ana kaynak olsun. Kalan hamle küçük ve tavanlı bir ek olsun. Kullanılan boosterdan XP verilmesin, çünkü bu paraya ilerleme satmak olur.
- Pass ve seviye sunucusuz ve tek telefonda yapılabilir. Ücretli pass ise mağaza ödeme sistemi ister. Takım, lig ve uzaktan sezon içeriği sunucu ister.

## 2. Player level and XP

### What the big match-3 games do

- None of the store pages or help pages read for Royal Match, Royal Kingdom, Candy Crush Saga, Toon Blast, Homescapes or Gardenscapes mentioned XP or an account level. The visible progress is the map level number. **FACT (absence on the pages read)**
- Royal Match gives one star per level, and stars are spent on castle tasks. **REPORTED** ([Scribd deconstruction snippet](https://www.scribd.com/document/1003133165/Full-Deconstruction-of-Royal-Match-s-Success))
- Candy Crush Saga moved away from scoring. From 1 Feb 2017 the score was raised to the one-star target once the goals were met. The wiki now says the exact score is no longer shown and the star meter tracks goals rather than points. Leftover moves used to turn into points ("Sugar Crush"). **REPORTED (fan wiki)** ([Candy Crush wiki: Score](https://candycrush.fandom.com/wiki/Points))

### The owner's three XP sources

The owner proposed XP from levels passed, from moves left, and from boosters used. The genre evidence on each:

| XP source | For | Against | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Level won** (base) | Simple, fair and predictable. It is the same rule the market leaders use for their pass counters: Royal Match keys, Toon Blast's 1 badge per win, Playrix season points. Bonuses for hard levels are standard (Royal Match and Royal Kingdom). | Players who reach the last level have nothing left to win, so their XP stops. Candy Crush's 2026 quest pass drew exactly this complaint from end-of-map players. With 1,000 levels this will happen to the most loyal players. | FACT (help pages) and REPORTED (King forum) |
| **Moves left** | Rewards skill and gives a reason to play well rather than just scrape through. | The leader moved the other way: Candy Crush dropped score-based stars, and Royal Match gives one star per level (REPORTED). Moves left is mostly luck on hard boards, and our levels are tuned to near-miss win rates, so most wins would earn almost nothing extra (ESTIMATE). If bought +5 moves leave moves unused, the purchase turns into XP, which is pay-to-progress (ESTIMATE). | REPORTED and ESTIMATE |
| **Boosters used** | Gives boosters a use beyond the level itself and a sink for the welcome-gift boosters. | It is XP for spending whenever boosters are bought. Spend-linked XP exists (Bingo Blitz gives more XP in pricier rooms and sells Double XP; Clash Royale gives XP for upgrades that cost gold; Monopoly GO's Tycoon Pass advances only with loyalty points from purchases). In a single-player offline game there is no ladder to make it unfair, but it cheapens the level number as a sign of skill, and players who avoid boosters fall behind (ESTIMATE). | REPORTED and ESTIMATE |

**Suggested rule (ESTIMATE):**

- Base XP for every level won.
- A bonus for hard levels, for a first-try win and for finishing a painting or room.
- Optionally a small bonus for moves left, with a cap, counting only the level's own moves and never bought +5 moves.
- No XP for boosters used.
- A non-level XP source for players at the end of the map, for example a daily restoration task.

What casual games kept: two tracks, a month-long season, a premium buy that is easy to justify, status cosmetics (gold name and frame), and a bank of rewards after the last step (Royal Match's Bonus Bank is like Clash Royale's 2019 bank, REPORTED).

Analyst view (REPORTED, [DoF 2022](https://www.deconstructoroffun.com/blog/2022/6/4/battle-passes-analysis)):
- A casual pass is mostly consumables with many free rewards.
- It is not meant to be the only purchase.
- It should limit very valuable items such as extra moves.
- It should stay simple, because complex missions lose casual players.

## 4. What the evidence says

### Adoption (REPORTED, analyst data; moderate strength)

- In 2019, 21% of the US iOS top-100 grossing games had a pass. ([GameRefinery, 17 Dec 2019](https://www.gamerefinery.com/battle-pass-trend-mobile-games/))
- In 2021, 51% of the US top-100 grossing games had one. ([GameRefinery, Jun 2021](https://www.gamerefinery.com/7-tips-to-create-and-improve-battle-pass/))
- In 2023, 66% of games in the top 20% had one, and about 66% of the casual games in GameRefinery's live-events tracker. ([GameRefinery, Oct 2023](https://www.gamerefinery.com/12-ways-to-take-battle-passes-to-the-next-level-in-mobile-games/))
- In 2021, about two thirds of the match-3 passes studied used "points per level won" as the counter, and the first stage was free in all of them. (GameRefinery, Jun 2021)

### Effect on retention and revenue

| Claim | Strength | Source |
|---|---|---|
| For most players, including payers, the pass works mainly to keep them engaged and coming back. Its free track gives everyone a reason to return, and buyers return so their purchase is not wasted. | REPORTED, analyst reasoning, no numbers | [DoF, Jun 2022](https://www.deconstructoroffun.com/blog/2022/6/4/battle-passes-analysis) |
| Passes convert players to paying well but have low spend per payer; most of their revenue is the upfront purchase. Tier skips earn very little. | REPORTED, analyst experience | DoF |

| Candy Crush Soda's choice of 7, 14 or 21 stages "improved monetization" and average spending. | REPORTED, no figures (weak) | [Gamigion](https://www.gamigion.com/?p=2672) |

| Royal Match earned over $1.4B from in-app purchases in 2025, with $0.17 IAP revenue per daily user against Candy Crush's $0.11. **Bottom line (ESTIMATE):**

- Every top casual puzzle game runs a pass, and the format is stable. That is strong evidence the pass pays for itself in these games.
- No public source gives a measured, causal effect on retention or conversion for a match-3 pass. The numbers available are adoption rates and whole-game revenue.
- Expect the pass to sell well to people who already pay. Expect some cannibalization of booster and coin packs. Measure it before trusting it.

## 5. Ideas that fit a museum and painting-restoration theme

**These are ideas, not facts. Each line is an ESTIMATE.**

1. **Exhibition Season (the pass).** A monthly "temporary exhibition" with a theme, such as a painter or a period. It has 30 steps, filled by "visitor tickets": 1 per level won, plus 1 or 2 for hard levels. This copies the counters used by Dream Games and Peak.
   - Free track: boosters, coins, short unlimited lives.
   - Premium track: more of the same, an exclusive frame style or plaque, and lives 5 to 8 (the genre standard).
   - Rewards left at the end are added automatically.
2. **Seasonal painting as the grand prize.** Finishing the season gives one extra public-domain painting for a "Special Exhibitions" wing. It stays after the season, so the reward is lasting rather than only consumables. Premium could add a gilded frame or a curator's note card.
3. **Donation box (a Bonus Bank).** After the last step, each further 10 tickets adds coins to a box with a cap. The box opens at season end for premium buyers. This copies Royal Match and Royal Kingdom.
4. **Restorer rank (player level).** XP follows the rule in section 2: wins, hard levels, first try, finished paintings and reopened rooms, with no booster XP. The ranks are titles: Apprentice, Assistant Restorer, Conservator, Master Restorer and Chief Curator. Each level-up gives a small gift (a booster or a refill of lives) and sometimes unlocks something: a tool skin (brush, magnifier), a museum-badge frame, or a mini-game.
5. **Apprentice's Notebook (onboarding pass).** A one-time free pass for about levels 1 to 50, before the monthly season unlocks. The genre unlocks its pass at levels 26 to 37, and DoF predicts more onboarding passes.
6. **Sources for players at the end of the map.** Daily restoration tasks (for example "clean 3 canvases" or "win 2 levels with no booster") that give tickets and XP after the last level. They could also run on replayed levels. This avoids the Candy Crush complaint.
7. **Double-ticket weekend.** A timed boost when progress stalls (GameRefinery tip 7).
8. **Later, after measuring: a second premium tier** (Toon Blast's Golden/Diamond, Candy Crush Soda's 7/14/21 stages). Start with one tier. GameRefinery suggests a 7-day trial season for a first pass.
9. **Pricing anchors from the genre.** $4.99 (Playrix) to $9.99 (Dream Games, Peak Golden); $19.99 only as a second tier. Reward value of 10 to 20 times the price (DoF). Keep extra moves out of the premium track, or small.

## 6. Needs a server or not

The game today has no server and no INTERNET permission.

| Idea | Works offline on one phone? | What breaks or needs a backend | Label |
|---|---|---|---|
| Exhibition Season, free track | Yes. Season dates can ship in the app or be computed from the device date. | Players can change the phone clock to jump seasons. In a single-player game this only affects that player. Seasons need content built into the app (art, reward tables), so new themes come with app updates. | ESTIMATE |
| Premium track | Partly. The purchase goes through the App Store or Google Play billing, which the build does not have yet. | Store billing has to be added. Checking receipts on a server is the usual guard against fake purchases; checking on the device alone is weaker. Ads and remote config would need the INTERNET permission. Before deciding, check whether Unity IAP or Play Billing needs INTERNET in this build. | ESTIMATE |
| Seasonal painting reward | Yes, if the paintings ship in the build or in a patch pack. | Downloading new paintings between updates needs a network and a file host. | ESTIMATE |
| Donation box (Bonus Bank) | Yes | None | ESTIMATE |
| Restorer rank / XP | Yes | It is lost if the phone is lost, unless there is a cloud save (iCloud, Google Play Games saved games or our own server). | ESTIMATE |
| Apprentice's Notebook | Yes | None | ESTIMATE |
| Daily restoration tasks | Yes (device date) | Clock changes can farm extra tasks; this is acceptable offline. | ESTIMATE |
| Double-ticket weekend | Yes if the schedule ships in the app | Turning it on live when players stall needs remote config, and so a network. | ESTIMATE |
| Teammate gifts, team chat notices (genre perks) | No | Needs a backend for teams and accounts. Leave these out. | ESTIMATE |
| Leaderboard or league pass (SimCity BuildIt style) | No | Needs a server for leaderboards and anti-cheat. | ESTIMATE |
| Live tuning of pass thresholds and prices | No | Needs analytics and remote config. Without them, tuning happens through app updates and the store's own reports. | ESTIMATE |
